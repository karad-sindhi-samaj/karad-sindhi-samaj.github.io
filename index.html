A little about us

Rooted in culture.
Growing together.

Karad Sindhi Samaj is a community for Sindhi families and individuals in Karad. This space is being built to help us stay connected, celebrate our traditions, share important updates, and make it easier for everyone to take part.

Our story belongs to all of us. We look forward to adding the people, memories, and milestones that make our Samaj special.
