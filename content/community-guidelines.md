# Community guidelines (draft)

> **Status: Draft for community review.** These principles are not official rules or regulations and should not be treated as adopted policy. The Samaj should review and approve any formal rules before publishing them as such.

## Our shared principles

1. **Respect and welcome:** Treat members, guests, and volunteers with courtesy. Make activities welcoming across generations and backgrounds.
2. **Cultural celebration:** Encourage the sharing and preservation of Sindhi heritage, language, traditions, and community history.
3. **Inclusive participation:** Share opportunities clearly and give members a fair chance to participate in community activities.
4. **Care for shared spaces:** Follow event arrangements, keep venues tidy, and use community resources responsibly.
5. **Privacy and consent:** Get permission before publishing personal details or identifiable photographs. Take particular care with children's images and information.
6. **Constructive communication:** Raise concerns respectfully through the Samaj's agreed process and avoid sharing unverified or private information.
7. **Transparent stewardship:** Communicate decisions and the handling of community funds through the appropriate Samaj process.

## Before adopting formal rules

The Samaj should agree on its membership, governance, meeting, financial, conduct, and dispute-resolution procedures through its own decision-making process. Record the approval date and responsible authority here when a formal version is adopted.
